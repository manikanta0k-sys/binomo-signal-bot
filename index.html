How signals work

Uses EMA, RSI, MACD histogram, momentum, higher-period trend, ATR, ADX, support/resistance. When they disagree, price data is outdated, candles are missing, or a signal has insufficient evidence, both cards show WAIT. It predicts the next full 1m candle and the next 4m span; it cannot know their future outcome.

Market feeds are separate and never blended as if they were the same price. Forex prices and exchange prices may differ from Binomo. Twelve Data can publish completed candles with a delay. The interface never claims to be monitoring your Binomo account.
